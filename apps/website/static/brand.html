04 · Rules

R1

Light mode is the default. Brand white is the default site surface. Dark mode is available and readable, but secondary.

R2

Emerald means network/action/ants. Use it for live network state, accents, pheromone paths, ant animation, and routing. Links on light surfaces use Primary 7.

R3

Clay means money. Use clay for $ANTS, $DIEM, USDC, fees, buy/burn, emissions, token pages, contracts, and economics sections.

R4

Use “fees,” not “revenues.” Economics copy should not imply equity, profit share, revenue share, or guaranteed return.

R5

Buttons are ink. Primary actions are #001E12 with 10px radius and brand-white text; secondary actions are white with a hairline border. No green pill buttons.

R6

No invisible UI. Pale mint on cream is not allowed. Background decoration — including the dot grid — must never reduce readability.

R7

Headings stay Medium. Geist 500 with -0.02em to -0.03em tracking. No 700+ display headings, no italics for emphasis — use color.

R8

Preserve content and SEO when redesigning. Visual updates should not rewrite page copy, titles, descriptions, canonical behavior, robots metadata, or structured content unless explicitly requested.
