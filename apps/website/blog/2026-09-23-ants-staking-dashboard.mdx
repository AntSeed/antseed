You can earn ANTS by using AI on Antseed and staking behind providers serving the network, or by becoming a provider. Our new staking dashboard brings them all together, helping you explore providers, manage your stake, and track your rewards.

<!-- truncate -->

## Ways to earn

When you use AI on Antseed, you can earn buyer rewards on [eligible paid usage](/docs/recognized-usage).

You can also stake ANTS into a provider's pool and earn staking rewards connected to its qualifying activity. You don't need to use that provider yourself to participate, and you can earn through both usage and staking.

The dashboard helps you understand what determines those rewards and choose how you want to participate.

![ANTS staking rewards](/img/blog/ants-staking-dashboard/what-determines-rewards.svg)

## Choosing who to back

Your choice of provider matters. Its qualifying usage helps determine the pool's staking rewards, while your staking power determines your share. That power depends on how much you stake and how long you lock it.

Other participants' stakes matter too, because you share the pool's rewards with them. A provider's activity and the amount of power already in its pool are both useful things to consider.

The dashboard lets you compare providers using settled volume, activity over time, active stake, and historical rewards. You can open a provider's profile to explore its requests, buyers, and models served where data is available.

![Sellers staking dashboard](/img/blog/ants-staking-dashboard/provider-profile.png)

## Your staking position

Choose a provider, an amount, and a lock period. Your staking power activates in the following epoch, and your position is represented by an [lANTS NFT](/docs/recognized-usage). You can split or merge locked positions without penalties, and move your stake between sellers at epoch boundaries.

The dashboard lets you track your positions, their provider pools, unlock dates, and rewards.

You can move your position to another provider while keeping the remaining lock. Withdrawing before it expires can burn part of your staked ANTS; after expiry, there's no early-exit penalty.

## Get started

1. Go to the AI VPN and open **Rewards** on the left bar menu, and click on **Manage staking**.
2. Or run `antseed ants`:

```text
antseed ants                # start on http://127.0.0.1:3119 and open the browser
antseed ants --no-open      # print the URL only
antseed ants --port 4000    # use another port
antseed ants --address 0x…  # pin the dashboard to one account
```

Explore the providers, review your available rewards, and choose whether to start a staking position. You can return to the dashboard to follow your positions and manage what you earn.
